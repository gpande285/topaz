What we do

Three stages, one production line

Printing

Offset and digital presses set up for short runs and long ones alike, with color matched and held across every reprint.

  • Offset and digital print
  • Spot color and process matching
  • Proofing before full runs

Lamination

Matte, gloss and soft-touch films bonded to printed stock for grip, shelf appeal and protection against moisture and scuffing.

  • BOPP, matte and gloss film
  • Soft-touch and anti-scuff finishes
  • Moisture and grease barriers

Paper products manufacturing

Cartons, boxes and custom paper packaging cut, folded and glued to spec, ready to pack straight off the line.

  • Folding cartons and rigid boxes
  • Die-cutting and creasing
  • Custom sizes and SKUs
